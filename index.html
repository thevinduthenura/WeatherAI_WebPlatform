Operational Integrity

Ethical Safeguards & Autonomous Actuation

Translating theoretical machine learning models into risk-aware greenhouse physical automation.

⚖️

Asymmetric Loss Function

An unpredicted frost event (false negative) causes blossom necrosis and total seasonal crop destruction, whereas a false warning incurs minor heating fuel cost. Controllers enforce a +1.5°C safety margin prioritizing frost recall over raw precision.

🌐

Geographic Domain Shift

Models trained on the continental climate of Szeged, Hungary cannot be deployed directly to tropical microclimates (such as Sri Lanka) without fine-tuning, as monsoonal humidity dynamics alter latent thermodynamic transfer rates.

🛡️

Sensor Fault Tolerance

An automated validation gateway monitors real-time sensor streams. If barometric pressure drops to 0.00 mbar or values freeze, the controller switches to a fail-safe persistence model to prevent physical hardware catastrophe.
